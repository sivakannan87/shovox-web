📐 Category C — Custom Components to Drawing

MADE TO DRAWING

Do you require components built to your exact proprietary design? International buyers can submit complete engineering packets or preliminary concept drawings. We coordinate with suitable manufacturing facilities possessing the required machine tonnage, spindle accuracy, and quality inspection infrastructure.

Information Buyers Can Submit for Feasibility & RFQ:

1. Technical Drawings

PDF engineering prints, DXF / DWG 2D layouts, and native/neutral STEP (.stp) 3D CAD files.

2. Product Photographs

Clear images of physical sample parts, cross-sections, wear points, or mating assembly contexts.

3. Part Numbers

Customer part numbers, revision levels, OEM cross-reference codes, or interchange references.

4. Material Requirements

Specific grades (AISI, DIN, EN, JIS), alloy chemistry, temper, heat treatment (HRC), or equivalent alternatives.

5. Dimensions & Geometry

Critical dimensions, thread pitches, bore sizes, chamfers, keyways, and wall thicknesses.

6. Tolerances & GD&T

Linear tolerances, geometric dimensioning, concentricity, perpendicularity, flatness, and runout limits.

7. Surface Finish

Roughness (Ra/Rz), electroplating (zinc, nickel, chrome), anodizing, black oxide, phosphating, or painting.

8. Quantity & Batches

Initial sampling quantity, pilot batch size, and projected monthly or annual blanket order volumes.

9. Delivery Schedule

Desired dispatch dates, target port of discharge, packaging specifications, and Incoterms (FOB, CIF, DAP).
