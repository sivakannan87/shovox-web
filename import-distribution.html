STRUCTURED SOURCING PROCESS

How We Facilitate Product Import

Navigating cross-border trade demands thorough verification, commercial discipline, and regulatory conformance.

🔍

Supplier Identification & Vetting

We assess international suppliers for factory credentials, export track records, technical capabilities, and compliance with global standards.

📜

Indian Regulatory Conformance

Verification of mandatory Indian import regulations, including BIS certifications, CDSCO registrations, DGFT guidelines, and customs classifications.

🚢

Commercial & Logistics Coordination

Comprehensive coordination covering proforma review, sample testing, freight forwarding, port customs clearance, and domestic delivery logistics.
