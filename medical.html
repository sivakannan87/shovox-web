OUR SOURCING ARCHITECTURE

Structured Export Sourcing Process

Every medical textile consignment is coordinated through rigorous supplier due diligence, laboratory parameter verification, and destination-country compliance validation.

01

Requirement Scoping

We review your institutional tender specifications, fiber blend ratios, GSM, wash cycles, and target regulatory standards.

02

Supplier Qualification

We shortlist verified Indian textile mills and converting units possessing audited spinning, dyeing, and hygienic stitching facilities.

03

Sampling & QA Review

Lab strike-offs, fabric tensile testing, shrinkage checks, and pre-shipment lot sampling conducted prior to bulk production run.

04

Export Logistics

Coordination of export documentation, fumigation, Certificate of Origin, and ocean/air freight forwarding to your destination port.
