AGRICULTURAL VALUE CHAIN

Integrated Agro Sourcing Workflow

From primary agricultural mandis to destination port delivery, our sourcing coordination ensures transparency, laboratory screening, and export-grade packing.

01

Crop Season & Origin Mapping

We align your procurement schedule with India's regional harvest seasons (Rabi & Kharif) for peak crop freshness and competitive pricing.

02

Cleaning & Sortex Grading

Shortlisting processing partners with modern destoning, magnetic separation, aspiration, and optical color sorting (Sortex) equipment.

03

Laboratory Screening

Facilitation of third-party accredited laboratory testing for moisture, curcumin, aflatoxin, pesticides, and microbial parameters.

04

Export Pack & Dispatch

Food-grade packaging, container stuffing, phytosanitary inspection, Certificate of Origin, and ocean shipping to global destination ports.
