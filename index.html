Duty-free access

Four markets, 0% import duty

India's trade agreements remove import duty on almost all of our products in these four countries.

UK

United Kingdom

India–UK CETA, in force 15 July 2026

99% of tariff lines at 0%. Clothing and home textiles were 8–12% before; leather goods now 0%.

UAE

United Arab Emirates

India–UAE CEPA, in force 1 May 2022

97% of tariff lines, covering 99% of India's exports by value.

OM

Oman

India–Oman CEPA, in force 1 June 2026

All 945 textile and apparel lines at 0% (was 5%), plus leather, engineering and agri items.

AU

Australia

India–Australia ECTA

All tariff lines at 0% from 1 January 2026.

How duty-free import works

*When the shipment carries a preferential certificate of origin and the goods meet the agreement's origin rules. We confirm the rate for your HS code in every quotation.

How we work

From enquiry to delivery

  1. 01

    Enquiry & quote

    Send your specification. We quote price, lead time, HS code and the duty rate in your country.

  2. 02

    Samples

    We send samples from the chosen mill. Production starts only after you approve them.

  3. 03

    Production & inspection

    We follow production and inspect the goods against your approved sample before packing.

  4. 04

    Shipping & documents

    We book freight and send the invoice, packing list and certificate of origin for 0% duty.
