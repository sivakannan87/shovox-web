About us

About SHOVOX INTERNATIONAL LLP

An export house in Bengaluru connecting buyers in the UK, the Gulf and Australia with Indian manufacturers.

Who we are

A focused Indian export house

SHOVOX INTERNATIONAL LLP is a Bengaluru-based trading company registered with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs. We export five product lines that India makes well: home and hospital textiles, workwear and uniforms, spices, leather goods and engineering hardware.

We focus on four markets where Indian goods now enter at 0% import duty: the United Kingdom, the UAE, Oman and Australia. We find and check the manufacturers, manage samples and quality inspections, and prepare the export documents, including the certificate of origin that gives our buyers the duty-free rate.

Our import line, bringing Gulf dates, dry fruits and short-supply spices to Indian wholesalers, will open later.

How we work

What buyers get from us

Checked suppliers

We visit or video-audit the mills and processors we work with, and keep their test reports on file.

Samples first

You approve samples before production. Bulk orders are checked against the approved sample before they ship.

Complete paperwork

Invoice, packing list, certificate of origin and any test reports your customs or customers need.

One contact

One person handles your order from quote to delivery, in English or Arabic.
