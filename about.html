COMPANY OVERVIEW

Connecting Indian Industry with Global Markets

SHOVOX INTERNATIONAL LLP is an India-based international trading business focused on product sourcing, import and distribution, export opportunities and collaboration with suitable suppliers and manufacturing partners.

Our business interests include hospital textiles, medical supplies, Indian foods and spices, precision-engineered components and electronics.

We aim to develop long-term business relationships through responsible sourcing, transparent communication and customer-focused commercial solutions.
